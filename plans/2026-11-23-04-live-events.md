# 2026-11-23-04-live-events.md

The apps say what their slow work did, as it happens. The tests (and anything else) are told, so
they never wait and look again.

## Why

The tests waited by looking again and again (`eventually()`, and a 1.5-second sleep) for queued
work to show up. When the work failed, nothing said so: the test sat until its timeout, 30 to 50
seconds, and then reported a missing button rather than the error.

## What changed

- **Live events in both apps** (`void/live`, each app's `src/live.ts`, watched at `/live`, admins
  only). Each queue announces its outcome on a topic:
  - service: `payouts` (queued, paid, failed), `events` (on sale, failed), `sales` (each Stripe
    event handled);
  - shop: `signups` (sent, failed), `event-emails` (batch, all sent, failed).
  - A failed event carries the error. The queue still throws, so Void tries again.
- **Clicks are counted before the redirect,** not after it, so they're there when the visitor
  arrives.
- **The tests:**
  - `watch()` subscribes to an app's topics before a step, then waits for the event;
  - each form step waits for its action's own response (`act()`, `press()`);
  - a failure ends the test at once: a "failed" event, a 5xx on any page, or a "[void] … failed"
    line in either app's log (`e2e/apps.ts` reads them);
  - `eventually()` and the sleeps are gone.
- **The shop's emails have a sender:** `email.from` in `apps/shop/void.config.ts`.

## Found along the way

- **The admin page crashed:** a query passed every ticket's id, past D1's 100 values in one
  query. It now uses a join.
- **The Partners tab listed organisers and venue contacts:** it lists affiliates only now. They're
  listed under Organisers and venues on the Events tab.
